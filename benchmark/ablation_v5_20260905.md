# Carrier-LaCAM v5 E5 ablation evidence

Date: 2026-09-05. All four runs use the frozen 77-case quick suite
(`a881292163ff2fcd2797cc83fddd8f9ebd12def79619586b940976bce07111c6`),
10 seconds per case, seed 0, unit weights, following allowed, and 14 workers.
Runs were executed sequentially on the same 16-physical-core host. Each
ablation was a separate Release build from the same final source snapshot;
no ablation flag or instance branch was added to production.

The exact removals were:

- **E1 off:** restore zero non-mandatory dummy assignment cost and remove the
  bottleneck threshold/masking; keep TransferKey, eligibility, custody and
  preparation semantics.
- **E2 off:** reduce the production timed-frame candidate budget from 8 to 1;
  retain timed coordination and the same successor oracle.
- **E4 off:** skip only the second same-controller improvement attempt; retain
  the common final replay and delivery path.

Interpretation is deliberately narrow. E1 and E2 do not demonstrate aggregate
quality benefit on this quick corpus; E1 removal is materially better in
aggregate, while E2 removal changes one plan hash without changing T or W.
E4 demonstrates strict improvement in 17 rows without losing a solved case,
at substantial runtime cost. These are feature-removal measurements, not a
claim that the quick suite proves general utility or optimality.
